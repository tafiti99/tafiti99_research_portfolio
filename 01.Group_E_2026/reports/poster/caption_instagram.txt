WHAT TRULY DRIVES MODERN CONTRACEPTIVE UPTAKE? 📊💊

Real-world evidence from 419 women of reproductive age in Mkuranga District, Tanzania.

KEY FINDINGS:
✨ Prevalence: 25.1% overall uptake (28.1% among married/cohabiting women). Injections lead at 45.7%, followed by implants at 27.6%.

✨ Knowledge Impact: High contraceptive knowledge nearly DOUBLES uptake (aPR = 1.89, P < 0.001).

✨ Partner Dynamics (The Decisive Factor):
• Frequent spousal discussion yields 2.7x HIGHER use (aPR = 2.67, P < 0.001).
• Even occasional dialogue more than doubles use (aPR = 2.39).
• Partner support increases prevalence by +85% (aPR = 1.85).

✨ The High-Parity Barrier:
Women with 3+ children had 32% LOWER use (aPR = 0.68), revealing contraceptive fatigue & misconceptions to address.

🧬 Methodology: Modified Poisson regression (Zou 2004) in R to prevent Odds Ratio inflation on common outcomes.

💡 Lesson: Involving men as supportive partners is the single most effective way to boost family planning uptake!
