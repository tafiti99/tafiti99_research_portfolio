What REALLY determines whether a woman uses modern contraception? 📊💊

We analyzed real data from 419 women of reproductive age in Mkuranga District, Tanzania—and the results challenge how family planning programs are traditionally run!

SWIPE TO SEE THE COMPLETE INFOGRAPHIC BREAKDOWN ➡️

Key Findings You Need to Know:
✨ 25.1% Current Uptake: 1 in 4 women currently uses modern contraception (injections 45.7%, implants 27.6%).
✨ Knowledge = Power: Women with high contraceptive knowledge were nearly 2x as likely to use modern methods (aPR = 1.89, P < 0.001).
✨ The Real Game-Changer? PARTNER COMMUNICATION.
Talking to a partner frequently resulted in 2.7x HIGHER contraceptive use! Even talking once or twice doubled the likelihood of use (aPR = 2.39).
✨ Partner Support: When men actively support family planning, uptake jumps by +85% (aPR = 1.85).
✨ The High-Parity Barrier: Women with 3+ children were 32% less likely to use modern contraception, revealing fear of side effects and misconceptions that health workers must address.

💡 THE BIG LESSON:
Family planning cannot remain a "women-only" conversation. When we involve men as supportive partners, contraceptive uptake skyrockets!

🧬 Analyzed using Modified Poisson Regression in R (Zou 2004) to avoid Odds Ratio distortion on common outcomes.
