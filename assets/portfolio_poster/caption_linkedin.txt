In medical and public health research, data analysis should never be a "black box" or a manual copy-paste exercise. Rigorous epidemiological research demands end-to-end reproducibility, sound biostatistical methodology, and strict data governance.

I built the TAFITI99 Research Portfolio to demonstrate production-grade biomedical data science in action.

🔍 WHAT THE REPOSITORY SHOWCASES:

1️⃣ Advanced Biostatistical Modeling:
• Modified Poisson Regression (Zou, 2004) with Huber-White robust sandwich variance (sandwich::vcovHC) to directly estimate Crude and Adjusted Prevalence Ratios (cPR / aPR).
• Why? Because when clinical outcomes exceed 10%, standard logistic regression Odds Ratios drastically inflate risk. Modified Poisson solves this dilemma with guaranteed convergence.

2️⃣ End-to-End Reproducible Research Architecture:
• Script-to-RDS checkpoint pipelines: Raw data → Cleaning & Deduplication → Exploration → Bivariate Analysis → Multivariable Modeling → Publication Objects.
• Zero manual hardcoding: Every number, confidence interval, and p-value in manuscripts flows dynamically from verified RDS checkpoints.

3️⃣ Automated Publishing with Quarto & R:
• Fully reproducible Word (.docx) and PDF generation via Quarto (.qmd).
• Publication-grade, ICMJE/APA-compliant tables generated programmatically via flextable.
• Visual forest plots and participant flow diagrams crafted with ggplot2.

4️⃣ Featured Research Project:
• "Assessment of Knowledge and Socio-Cultural Factors Affecting Modern Contraceptive Use among Women of Reproductive Age in Mkuranga District, Tanzania" (N = 419). Complete pipeline from raw KoboToolbox export to final thesis Chapter 4.
