Here is what this open-source portfolio brings to the table:

✨ Advanced Biostatistics in R:
Moving beyond basic tests to advanced methods like Modified Poisson Regression (Zou 2004) with robust sandwich standard errors to estimate true Relative Risk and Prevalence Ratios.

✨ 100% Reproducible Pipelines:
No more messy Excel copy-pasting! Every project uses clean R scripts with cached RDS checkpoints, ensuring 100% traceability from raw data to final conclusions.

✨ Automated Publishing with Quarto:
Generating publication-ready Word & PDF documents, complete with APA-standard tables (flextable) and custom ggplot2 forest plots.

✨ Featured Real-World Project:
A complete cross-sectional study of 419 women in Mkuranga District on modern contraceptive use, examining knowledge, partner communication, and socio-cultural drivers.

🛠️ Built with: R, Tidyverse, Quarto, ggplot2, sandwich, and Git.
